IntelliMathKids Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the IntelliMathKids Android app ("the app") handles information. The app is a maths learning app designed for children. We take children's privacy seriously, and the app is built so that it does not collect personal information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app does not ask for a name, email address, phone number, location, photos, contacts, or any other personal data, and it does not require an account or sign-in.

Information stored on the device

The app saves your child's best (high) scores for quizzes and puzzles so they can try to beat them. These scores are stored only on the device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and they are deleted when the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared.

Internet access, advertising and tracking

  • The app works fully offline and does not request internet access.
  • The app contains no advertising and no in-app purchases.
  • The app does not use the Android advertising ID.
  • The app does not include analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs that collect data.

Text-to-speech

The app can read numbers and times tables aloud using the text-to-speech engine already installed on the device. The text being spoken is generated inside the app and is not sent to us. The speech engine is provided by your device or its manufacturer and is covered by their own privacy policy.

Permissions

The app does not request any special Android permissions.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for children and is designed to comply with the Google Play Families Policy and children's privacy laws such as COPPA (United States) and GDPR (European Union). Because the app collects no personal information, no personal information from children is collected, used, or disclosed.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above.
